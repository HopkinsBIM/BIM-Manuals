Clash Coordination Matrix

The matrix defines which discipline pairs are clash-tested, at what priority and clearance tolerance. The coordination sheets show who is accountable depending on whether the architect acts as Lead Design Consultant or as Architect only.

02 — Priority & Tolerance
Clash Priority
P1
Critical / Hard — Life-safety, structural conflict. 0 mm tolerance.
P2
Major / Hard — Physical overlap or clearance breach. Resolve in cycle.
P3
Minor / Soft — Clearance or maintenance-zone breach. Tracked.
N/A
No meaningful interface — no test required.
Clearance Tolerance
0 mm — Hard clash; any geometric overlap flagged.
25 mm — Insulation / cover / installation clearance.
50 mm — Service-to-service running clearance.
150 mm — Electrical containment from wet services.
— — Governed by design zone / authority requirement.

04 — Give-Way / Resolution Hierarchy

Highest priority stays put; lower gives way.

#
Element
Basis
1
Structure (ST)
Moves last — reroute services, never the frame.
2
Gravity drainage (PH) & Civil (CV)
Fixed invert levels / gradients.
3
Vertical transportation (VT)
Fixed shaft, pit and running clearances.
4
Large ductwork (ME)
Bulky, low flexibility; other services route around it.
5
Fire mains & sprinkler (FF)
Coordinated grid; limited flexibility.
6
Pressurised water pipes (PH)
More flexible than gravity / ducts.
7
Cable trays / busbar (EL — major)
Reroutable within containment strategy.
8
Comms & data containment (ICT)
Light, flexible; keep EMI separation from EL.
9
Conduit / final connections (EL — minor)
Most flexible; gives way first.
10
Architecture (AR)
Absorbs residual via bulkheads / ceiling drops.
05 — Coordination Scenarios
Scenario 1 — As LDC
  • Architect hosts & maintains the federated model
  • Architect runs clash detection across all pairs
  • Architect is Accountable (A) for every pair
  • Architect chairs coordination & tracks to closure
Scenario 2 — As Architect Only
  • Federated model hosted by appointed BIM Coordinator
  • Architect runs detection only on AR-involving pairs
  • Accountability sits with the appointed lead
  • Architect attends coordination & closes own items
Assumptions: Generic building project — discipline list to be trimmed per project scope. Tolerances are indicative — align to the project BEP and authority requirements. Matrix is symmetric; only the upper-right triangle is populated.
