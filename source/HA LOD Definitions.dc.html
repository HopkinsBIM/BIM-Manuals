LOD Definitions · Level of Development HA BIM Manual Rev C · BIM Forum LOD Spec 2025

Level of Development (LOD) describes the content and reliability of a BIM model element at a given stage. LOD is used to set expectations for what information is contained in model elements and what can be inferred from them. These definitions apply to all Hopkins Architects projects and are aligned with the BIM Forum LOD Specification 2025 and ISO 19650.

Visual Reference — LOD Progression by Discipline

LOD Definitions

100

Conceptual / Symbolic

Generic representation — does not satisfy LOD 200 requirements

AIA Definition

The Model Element may be graphically represented with a symbol or other generic representation but does not satisfy the requirements for LOD 200. Information related to the element (e.g., cost per square foot) can be derived from other Model Elements.

BIM Forum Interpretation

LOD 100 elements are not geometric representations. Examples include information attached to other model elements or symbols showing the existence of a component, but not its shape, size, or precise location. Any information derived from LOD 100 elements must be considered approximate.

200

Approximate Geometry

Generic placeholder with approximate size, shape, location and orientation

AIA Definition

The Model Element is generically and graphically represented with approximate quantity, size, shape, location, and orientation. Non-geometric information may also be attached.

BIM Forum Interpretation

At this LOD, elements are generic placeholders. They may be recognisable as the components they represent, or they may be volumes for space reservation. Any information derived from LOD 200 elements must be considered approximate.

300

Modelled As Designed

Specific geometry — measurable quantity, size, shape, location and orientation

Most common target
AIA Definition

The Model Element, as designed, is graphically represented such that its quantity, size, shape, location, and orientation can be measured. Non-geometric information may also be attached.

BIM Forum Interpretation

Quantity, size, shape, location, and orientation can be measured directly from the model without referring to non-modelled information such as notes or dimension callouts. The project origin is defined and the element is located accurately.

350

As Designed + Interfaces

LOD 300 plus coordination of adjacent and dependent elements

AIA Definition

As LOD 300, plus interfaces with adjacent or dependent Model Elements can be measured. Parts necessary for coordination, such as support and connections, are included.

BIM Forum Interpretation

Parts necessary for coordination with nearby or attached elements are modelled, including support and connections. Quantity, size, shape, location, and orientation can be measured directly from the model.

400

Fabrication & Assembly

Detail sufficient for fabrication, assembly, and installation

AIA Definition

The Model Element is graphically represented with detail sufficient for fabrication, assembly, and installation. Non-geometric information may also be attached.

BIM Forum Interpretation

A LOD 400 element is modelled with sufficient detail and accuracy for the fabrication of the represented component. All geometric attributes can be measured directly from the model.

500

As-Constructed / Field Verified

Existing or as-constructed condition via observation and field verification

AIA Definition

The Model Element is a graphic representation of an existing or as-constructed condition, developed through a combination of observation, field verification, or interpolation. The level of accuracy shall be noted or attached.

BIM Forum Interpretation

LOD 500 relates to field verification and is not an indication of higher geometry progression. This Specification does not define or illustrate it further. Refer to BIM Forum LOD Specification 2025, Part I.
